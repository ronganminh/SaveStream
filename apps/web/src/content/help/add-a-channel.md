## Add it

1. Open **Channels** and choose **Add channel**.
2. Enter the TikTok username (with or without `@`) or the profile link, for example `https://www.tiktok.com/@yourchannel`.
3. Confirm. The channel appears in your list and SaveStream begins checking whether it is live.

Usernames are 2–24 characters: letters, numbers, periods and underscores. A username and a profile link for the same creator count as one channel, so you can't add it twice. Only TikTok is supported today.

## How many channels you can add

Free accounts can monitor {{free_channels}}. After a purchase you can monitor {{pro_channels}}. Deleted channels don't count toward the limit.

## Pause, resume or delete

- **Monitoring** switch on the channel page: turn it off to pause checks, on to resume.
- **Remove channel** in the channel menu deletes it. Recordings you already have stay in **Recordings** until you delete them or they expire.

## Channel statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Offline / Waiting | Being checked; not live right now. |
| Live | The creator is live. If it isn't recording, the channel shows why. |
| Recording | A recording is running in the cloud. |
| Paused | Paused by you, paused because there weren't enough credits, or restricted. |
| Error | Checks failed several times in a row and were stopped. Check the username, then turn **Monitoring** back on. |
