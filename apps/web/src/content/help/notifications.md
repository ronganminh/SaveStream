## Where notifications appear

Notifications appear under the bell icon in the app and on the **Notifications** page. The SaveStream mobile app can also show them as push notifications. Email notifications for recordings are not available yet.

## What you are notified about

- A monitored channel goes live.
- A recording starts, is ready, fails or is stopped early.
- A LIVE was missed because all recording slots were busy.
- A recording is about to expire.
- Your credits ran out.
- A credit purchase completed.

## Choose what you receive

In **Settings → Notifications** you can turn the *recording started*, *recording ready* and *recording ended* notifications on or off. The live alert can be turned off per channel on the channel page.
