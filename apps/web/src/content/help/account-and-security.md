## Sign in

Sign in with your email and password. Your email must be verified first — use the link in the verification email.

## Forgot or change your password

Use **Forgot password** on the sign-in page, or **Settings → Security → Email password reset link** when signed in. The link in the email is valid for 30 minutes. Setting a new password signs you out of every device.

## Sessions and devices

**Settings → Security** lists the browsers and devices signed in to your account, with a partial IP address and when each was last active. You can sign out a single session, or choose **Sign out everywhere** to end all of them, including the current one.

## Your profile

You can change your display name in **Settings → Account**. To change the email address on your account, contact {{support_email}}.

## Export your data

**Settings → Account → Download data export** saves a copy of your account data as a JSON file.

## Delete your account

**Settings → Account → Delete account**, then type `DELETE` to confirm. The account is disabled and signed out immediately, and your data is removed as described in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy). This can't be undone; download any recordings you want to keep first.

## Keep your account safe

Use a password you don't use elsewhere. If you think someone else has accessed your account, reset your password and email {{support_email}}.
