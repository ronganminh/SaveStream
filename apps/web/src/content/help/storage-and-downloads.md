## Watch in the browser

Open a recording in **Recordings** and press play. Recordings keep TikTok's original vertical format and quality. Playback uses a short-lived secure link, so a page left open for a long time may ask you to press play again.

## Download

Choose **Download video**. The file saves as an MP4 named after the channel and the time the LIVE started, for example `yourchannel_2026-10-14_2106.mp4`. Downloading doesn't use credits.

## How long recordings are kept

Recordings are stored for {{paid_retention}} on accounts that have bought credits, and {{trial_retention}} on trial-only accounts, counted from when the recording was created. Each recording shows its expiry date, and a banner appears when only a few days are left. You also get a notification about a day before a recording expires.

After that the recording and its file are deleted permanently. Download anything you want to keep longer.

## Delete a recording

Open the recording, choose **Delete** and confirm. Deletion is permanent. Recordings that are still running or processing can't be deleted until they finish.

## Recording statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Recording | The LIVE is being recorded right now. |
| Processing | Waiting to start, finishing, or uploading the file. |
| Ready | Ready to watch and download. |
| Stopped | Stopped early; the part recorded so far is saved. |
| Missed | The LIVE ended while waiting for a free recording slot. Not charged. |
| Failed | No usable video was saved. Not charged. |
