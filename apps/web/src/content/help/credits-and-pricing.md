## What a credit buys

Recording time is paid with credits at {{credit_rate}}, with {{minimum_credits}}. Checking channels while they are offline is free — credits are only used while a LIVE is being recorded.

## How a recording is charged

1. **Reserve.** When a recording starts, SaveStream reserves enough credits for its maximum length. Reserved credits show as _held_ in **Usage**.
2. **Charge.** When it ends, you are charged for the real length of the saved video.
3. **Release.** The rest of the reservation goes back to your available balance. In your history this appears as _Released N held credits_.

The rate is fixed when a recording starts, so a price change never affects a recording that is already running.

## What is not charged

- Recordings that fail with no usable video.
- LIVEs that were **Missed** because all your recording slots were busy.
- Checking offline channels.

A recording that ends early — because the LIVE ended or you pressed **Stop recording** — is charged for the time that was recorded.

## Buying credits

Credits come in one-time packs; there is no subscription and nothing renews. See the [Pricing](/pricing) page for the current packs and prices, and [PayPal payments and refunds](/help/paypal-and-refunds) for how checkout works.

## Free trial credits

New accounts receive {{signup_credits}} — {{trial_minutes}} of recording — after verifying their email, once per account. Use them to try automatic recording on a real LIVE, one recording at a time. When they are used up, buy a pack to keep recording. Trial credits can't be refunded or exchanged for money.

## Do credits expire?

No. Unused credits stay in your balance until you use them.
