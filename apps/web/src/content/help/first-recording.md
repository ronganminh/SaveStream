SaveStream watches the TikTok channels you add and records their LIVEs on our servers. Nothing needs to stay open on your computer or phone.

## 1. Create your account

Sign up with your email address and confirm you are 18 or older and accept the Terms. Open the link in the verification email to activate the account. New accounts receive {{signup_credits}} once the email is verified.

## 2. Add a channel

Open **Channels** and choose **Add channel**. Enter a TikTok username such as `@yourchannel` or a profile link like `tiktok.com/@yourchannel`. Only add channels you own, manage, or have clear permission to record — see [What you may record](/help/acceptable-use).

From that moment SaveStream checks the channel automatically. Checking an offline channel is free.

## 3. Turn on automatic recording

Automatic cloud recording unlocks after your first credit purchase. Buy a pack in **Usage & Billing**, then open the channel and switch **Auto-record** on. The next time the channel goes live, recording starts on its own.

See [How automatic recording works](/help/automatic-recording) for the details.

## 4. Watch or download the recording

When the LIVE ends, the recording is processed and appears in **Recordings**. Play it in the browser or choose **Download video** to save the MP4 file. Recordings are kept for a limited time — see [Storage and downloads](/help/storage-and-downloads).
