SaveStream may suspend or close an account when we reasonably believe it was used in a way our [Terms of Use](/terms) or [Acceptable Use Policy](/acceptable-use) don't allow. The most common reason is recording TikTok LIVEs without the channel owner's permission. Another is a copyright notice we have upheld.

## Suspended

A suspension is temporary while we review a report.

- **Signing in.** When you sign in you see the reason, the date and a reference such as `SR-1040`. We also email you.
- **Channels and recordings.** Channel monitoring and new recordings are paused, and recordings in progress are stopped.
- **What's kept.** Your recordings, credits and history are kept, but you can't open them while the account is suspended.
- **When it's lifted.** The channels the suspension paused are monitored again. Recordings that were stopped are not restarted.

## Closed (terminated)

We close an account for serious or repeated violations, such as repeated copyright infringement.

- You can no longer sign in, and monitoring has stopped.
- Recordings are not deleted early. They expire at the end of their normal retention period.
- If you have unused paid credits, contact us. Requests are reviewed under the [Refund Policy](/refund).

## How to appeal

If you think this is a mistake, email **{{support_email}}**:

- use the subject **"Appeal"** followed by the reference shown on the page or in the email, for example _Appeal SR-1040_;
- explain how you own the channel, manage it, or have the creator's permission to record it;
- if you can, include proof such as a message from the creator or access to the channel's account.

We usually reply within {{support_response_time}}. Required account emails (sign-in, password reset) still reach you during a suspension.
