## Failed

The recording couldn't produce a usable video — for example the stream became unavailable or the file couldn't be saved. **Failed recordings are not charged**: the credits held for them are released. The recording page shows the reason.

SaveStream already retries some temporary problems automatically. There is no retry button for you to press; if failures keep happening for a channel, email {{support_email}} with the recording link.

## Stopped or ended early

If the LIVE ended sooner than expected or you pressed **Stop recording**, everything recorded until then is saved and can be watched and downloaded. You are charged only for the recorded time.

## Ended at the length limit

A single recording runs for up to 4 hours, or as long as your available credits cover when it starts, whichever is shorter. If your credits ran out, you receive a notification; buy a pack to keep recording future LIVEs.

## Missed

The LIVE ended while it was waiting for a free recording slot because your account was already recording {{parallel_recordings}}. Missed LIVEs are not charged.

## Get help

Email {{support_email}} with the recording link and what you expected to happen.
