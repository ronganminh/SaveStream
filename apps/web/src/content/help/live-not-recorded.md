---
title: A LIVE wasn't recorded — what to check
category: troubleshooting
summary: Open the channel first — its status usually tells you exactly why a LIVE was not recorded.
updated: 2026-10-16
order: 1
---

SaveStream records a LIVE automatically only when the channel is being monitored, **Auto-record** is on and your account can pay for the recording. If a LIVE is missing from **Recordings**, one of these is almost always the reason.

## Your email isn't verified yet

Your {{trial_minutes}} can only record once your email is verified. Open the link in the verification email (you can resend it from **Settings → Account**). Monitoring and LIVE alerts work in the meantime.

## Auto-record is off for the channel

Open the channel and check the **Auto-record** switch. Each channel has its own switch.

## Your free minutes or credits ran out

With no available credits, automatic recording can't start — whether your free minutes are used up or a pack is spent. A recording that was running when the credits ran out stops there and is saved. Buy a pack in **Usage & Billing**; channels that show **Paused** because there weren't enough credits resume automatically.

## Monitoring is paused or shows an error

Paused channels are not checked. A channel shows **Error** after several failed checks in a row — usually because the username changed or the account is private or restricted. Fix the username if needed, then turn **Monitoring** back on.

## All your recording slots were busy

After a purchase your account records {{parallel_recordings}} at a time; on free minutes it records one. A LIVE that ended while waiting for a free slot is marked **Missed** and isn't charged.

## The LIVE was very short

Channels are checked about every 30 seconds. A LIVE that ends within the first few seconds may finish before recording starts.

## Still missing?

Email {{support_email}} with the channel name and the approximate start time of the LIVE.
