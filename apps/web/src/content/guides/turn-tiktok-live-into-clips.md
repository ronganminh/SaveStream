A one-hour LIVE often contains a handful of moments that would work on their own: a great answer to a question, a funny reaction, a product demo, a story your audience loved. Cut into short vertical clips, those moments can reach people who never saw the LIVE, on TikTok and on other short-video platforms.

This guide walks through a simple, repeatable workflow, from getting the recording to posting the clip. It is written for **your own LIVEs**, or LIVEs you manage with the creator's permission. Clipping and reposting someone else's stream without permission is not allowed and can lead to takedowns.

## Step 1: Get a good copy of the LIVE

Everything starts with a clean recording. You have a few options:

- **A recording from your streaming software**, if you went live from a computer with local recording on. This usually has the best quality.
- **A screen recording** made on another device while the LIVE was on.
- **A cloud recording.** SaveStream records the channels you add automatically when they go live, in TikTok's original vertical format. Download it from **Recordings** as an MP4.

Vertical source video is a big advantage: clips for vertical feeds need no cropping, so you keep the full frame.

## Step 2: Find the highlights

Rewatching a full LIVE to find clips is the slowest part. These habits make it faster:

- **Note timestamps during the LIVE.** Ask a moderator or teammate to jot down the time of anything that stands out.
- **Watch at higher speed.** Most players can play at 1.5× or 2×; slow down when something interesting starts.
- **Look for self-contained moments.** A good clip makes sense without context: a question and its answer, a reveal, a tip, a reaction.
- **Use your comments.** Moments where the chat lit up are usually the ones worth clipping.

Aim for a short list of strong moments rather than many weak ones.

## Step by step

Turn one highlight into a finished clip:

1. **Open the recording in an editor.** Any video editor works, from the built-in editor on your phone to a desktop editing program.
2. **Trim to the moment.** Cut in just before the action starts and out as soon as the point lands. Shorter is usually better; remove pauses and filler.
3. **Keep it vertical.** Export at a 9:16 aspect ratio. If the source is a vertical LIVE recording, no cropping is needed.
4. **Add captions.** Many people watch without sound. Add on-screen captions or use your editor's or the platform's automatic captions, and check them for mistakes.
5. **Add a hook.** Put a short line of text at the top in the first second that tells viewers why to keep watching.
6. **Check the audio.** Remove background music you don't have the rights to use, and balance your voice so it is clear.
7. **Export and post.** Export as MP4 and post natively on each platform, with a caption that invites people to your next LIVE.

## Platform notes

Short-video platforms change their rules often, so check each one's current requirements before you post:

- **Length limits** differ by platform and change over time. Check the current maximum in each app.
- **Watermarks**: some platforms show clips with another app's watermark less often. Export a clean version from your original recording rather than reposting a downloaded post.
- **Music**: music that played during your LIVE may be licensed only for TikTok. Replace it with music from the platform's own library, or remove it.
- **Captions and text placement**: keep important text away from the edges where the app's buttons and captions sit.

## Rights and permissions

Clipping is only safe when you have the right to use everything in the clip:

- **Your own content** — your voice, your face, your products — is yours to use.
- **Guests and co-hosts** should agree before you post clips featuring them.
- **Viewer comments and usernames** shown on screen are other people's content; blur or crop them if you are unsure.
- **Music, videos or images** shown during the LIVE may belong to someone else.

SaveStream only records channels you own, manage, or have clear permission to record. See [What you may record](/help/acceptable-use).

## Build a routine

A routine turns clipping into a habit instead of a chore:

1. Record every LIVE automatically, so you always have the source.
2. Within a day of each LIVE, download the recording and pick out a few highlights.
3. Edit and schedule the clips across the week, which keeps your feeds active between LIVEs.
4. Track which clips bring people to your next LIVE, and look for more moments like those.

## Frequently asked questions

### What format should my clips be?

Vertical 9:16 MP4 works on all the major short-video platforms. Recordings of TikTok LIVEs are already vertical, so you can trim them without cropping.

### How long should a clip be?

Long enough to make one point, and no longer. Many strong clips are under a minute. Each platform sets its own maximum length, so check the current limit in the app.

### Can I clip LIVEs from other creators?

Only with their permission. Reposting other people's streams without permission can lead to copyright claims and account penalties, and SaveStream only records channels you own, manage or are allowed to record.

### Do I need special software?

No. The built-in editor on most phones can trim and export clips. Desktop editors add features such as multi-track audio and precise captions.
