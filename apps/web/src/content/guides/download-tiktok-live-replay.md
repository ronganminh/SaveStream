You finished a great LIVE and now you want the video: to rewatch it, cut highlights, or keep it as a record. Whether you can still get it depends on what happened _before_ and _during_ the stream. This guide covers the options you have after a LIVE has ended, and how to set things up so the next one is saved without any effort.

As always: these steps are for **your own LIVEs**, or LIVEs you manage or have the creator's permission to save. Downloading and reposting other people's streams is not allowed on SaveStream and can break TikTok's rules. See [What you may record](/help/acceptable-use).

## First, check whether TikTok kept a replay

TikTok offers some creators a replay of their past LIVEs in its own creator tools and LIVE settings. Whether you see this option, how long replays stay available, and whether you can download them depends on your account, region and current TikTok features, so check the LIVE section of the TikTok app and TikTok's own help center for what applies to you.

If a replay is available and has a download or save option, use it. That is the most direct way to get a copy of a LIVE that has already ended. If there is no replay, a LIVE that was not recorded while it happened usually can't be recovered later.

## If you recorded the LIVE yourself

If you or someone on your team screen-recorded the LIVE, or you streamed from a computer with local recording turned on, the file is already on that device:

- **Phone screen recordings** are saved to the photo library or gallery.
- **Streaming software recordings** are saved to the folder set in its recording or output settings.

Copy the file somewhere safe as soon as you can. Phones and laptops get lost, replaced or wiped, and long videos are often the first thing people delete when storage runs low.

## If SaveStream recorded the LIVE

If the channel was added to SaveStream with **Auto-record** on, the LIVE was recorded in the cloud while it happened and is waiting in your account.

## Step by step

How to save a LIVE that SaveStream recorded:

1. **Sign in.** Open SaveStream and go to **Recordings**.
2. **Find the LIVE.** Recordings are listed newest first with the channel name, start time and length. A recording marked **Ready** can be played and downloaded.
3. **Check it in the browser.** Open the recording and press play to make sure it is the one you want.
4. **Download the MP4.** Choose **Download video**. The file is named after the channel and start time, for example `yourchannel_2026-10-14_2106.mp4`. Downloading doesn't use credits.
5. **Store it safely.** Move the file to a drive or cloud storage you control. Recordings are kept in SaveStream for {{paid_retention}} on accounts that have bought credits ({{trial_retention}} on trial-only accounts) and then deleted permanently.

## Why a LIVE might not have been saved

If you expected a recording and can't find it, the most common reasons are:

- **Auto-record wasn't on** for that channel, the account email wasn't verified yet, or the free minutes or credits had run out.
- **The account ran out of credits**, so new recordings couldn't start.
- **All recording slots were busy.** An account records {{parallel_recordings}} at the same time; a LIVE that ended while waiting for a free slot is marked **Missed** and isn't charged.
- **The LIVE was very short** and ended within seconds of starting.

[A LIVE wasn't recorded — what to check](/help/live-not-recorded) explains each case and how to fix it.

## Make sure the next LIVE is saved

The surest way to always have your replays is to record every LIVE as it happens, without depending on remembering to press a button:

- **Add every channel you go live on** to SaveStream, and switch **Auto-record** on for each one.
- **Keep credits topped up.** Credits come in one-time packs and never expire, so a spare pack means a long LIVE is never cut short. Recording is charged at {{credit_rate}}.
- **Turn on live alerts** so you get a notification when a channel goes live and when the recording is ready.
- **Download regularly.** Set a weekly habit to download new recordings and move them to long-term storage.

## Organizing your saved LIVEs

Once you start saving every stream, the files add up. A simple system saves time later:

- **One folder per channel**, with sub-folders by month.
- **Keep the original file name** with the date and time, and add a short note if a LIVE had a special guest, launch or announcement.
- **Keep a short log** — a spreadsheet with the date, topic and highlights with timestamps makes it easy to find moments for clips later.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I download a TikTok LIVE after it has ended?

Only if a copy exists: a replay kept by TikTok for your account, a recording you made yourself, or a cloud recording made while the LIVE was happening. A LIVE that nobody recorded usually can't be recovered.

### Is downloading my recording free?

Yes. In SaveStream, credits pay for recording time only. Watching and downloading your recordings doesn't use credits.

### How long does SaveStream keep my recordings?

Recordings are kept for {{paid_retention}} on accounts that have bought credits and {{trial_retention}} on trial-only accounts, counted from when the recording was created. Each recording shows its expiry date, and you are notified before it expires.

### Can I save LIVEs from a channel I don't own?

Only if you manage that channel or the creator has given you clear permission to record it. Adding a channel to SaveStream confirms you have that right.
